Land Rover

Land Rover offers a range of luxury SUVs and 4x4 vehicles, including the Range Rover, Discovery, and Defender models. The brand is known for its off-road capabilities and has a rich history dating back to its inception in 1948. Land Rover is a subsidiary of Jaguar Land Rover, which is owned by Tata Motors. The brand has a strong presence in the Indian market, with a variety of models available, including the Range Rover Evoque, Range Rover Velar, Range Rover Sport, Range Rover, Discovery Sport, Discovery, and Defender. The prices for Land Rover cars in India start at ₹63.37 Lakh and go up to ₹4.25 crore, with the latest model, the Range Rover Velar, priced at ₹83.90 Lakh.

Bugatti

Founding: Bugatti was established in 1909 in Molsheim, Alsace, by Ettore Bugatti, an Italian-born industrial designer. The brand quickly gained a reputation for its innovative engineering and artistic design. Notable Models: Some of the most famous Bugatti models include the Type 35, known for its racing success, the luxurious Type 41 Royale, and the iconic Type 57 Atlantic, which is celebrated for its art deco design and rarity.

Rollce Royce

Overview of Rolls-Royce Motor Cars Rolls-Royce Motor Cars Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of BMW AG, established in 2003. The company is headquartered at the Goodwood plant in West Sussex, England. Rolls-Royce is renowned for producing high-end luxury vehicles, including models like the Phantom, Ghost, Cullinan SUV, and the Spectre, which is their first electric car. The brand is often regarded as a symbol of luxury and excellence, with the name "Rolls-Royce" becoming synonymous with superlative quality in the automotive industry.

LAMBORGHINI

Lamborghini (officially Automobili Lamborghini S.p.A. and colloquially Lambo) is an Italian manufacturer of luxury sports cars and SUVs based in Sant'Agata Bolognese. The company is owned by the Volkswagen Group through its subsidiary Audi. Ferruccio Lamborghini (1916–1993), an Italian manufacturing magnate, founded Automobili Ferruccio Lamborghini S.p.A. in 1963 to compete with Ferrari. The company was noted for using a rear mid-engine, rear-wheel drive layout. Lamborghini grew rapidly during its first decade, but sales plunged in the wake of the 1973 worldwide financial downturn and the oil crisis.

TATA

The Tata Group is a prominent Indian multinational conglomerate founded in 1868, known for its diverse business operations across various sectors, including steel, automobiles, IT, and hospitality. Overview The Tata Group is one of India's largest and oldest business conglomerates, established by Jamsetji Tata in 1868. Headquartered in Mumbai, it operates in over 100 countries across six continents and comprises more than 30 companies in various sectors, including automobiles, steel, chemicals, consumer products, energy, and hospitality. As of 2025, the group has a combined market capitalization of approximately ₹37.84 trillion (US$436 billion).
